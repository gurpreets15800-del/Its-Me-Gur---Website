A Beverage Is Never Just a Recipe

A good beverage may start with a recipe, but creating a successful product requires much more than knowing what ingredients to put together.

Beverage development has taught me that creating something new is a balance between creativity and practicality.

A recipe can taste great during a trial and still fail when it reaches a real café environment. That is where product development becomes interesting.

Flavour is only the beginning.

The first question is usually simple: does it taste good?

But that cannot be the only question. A product also needs to be consistent, practical to prepare and understandable to the customer.

Ingredients, preparation methods, serving format and presentation all become part of the final product.

The customer is part of the equation.

Product development is not about creating something that only the developer likes.

You have to understand why someone would order it, what they expect from it and whether the product gives them a reason to come back.

Sometimes a complicated idea can be simplified and become stronger. Sometimes a small change in flavour, texture or presentation can completely change the experience.

Then comes the business side.

A beverage also has to make sense operationally.

Cost, ingredient availability, preparation time, wastage, equipment, staff training and scalability all matter.

The best product is not necessarily the most complicated one. It is the one that delivers a strong experience while remaining practical enough to execute consistently.

Innovation is a process.

For me, beverage innovation is less about trying to create something completely unheard of and more about continuously asking better questions.

Can this be better? Can it be simpler? Can it be more consistent? Can the customer experience be improved?

That mindset is what turns a recipe into a product.

— GURPREET SINGH
