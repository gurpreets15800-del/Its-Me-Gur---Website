Why I Started Looking Beyond the Coffee Cup

Coffee gave me a foundation, but curiosity slowly pushed me to explore everything that happens beyond the cup.

Coffee has been a major part of my professional journey. It taught me about flavour, technique, consistency and the importance of paying attention to small details.

But over time, I realised that my curiosity was becoming bigger than coffee itself.

Coffee was the starting point.

Working behind the bar teaches you to understand a product through your hands. You learn how ingredients behave, how small changes affect flavour and how important consistency is.

Those lessons eventually started making me curious about other beverages and products.

Curiosity changes the way you work.

I started asking different questions.

Why does one product become popular while another disappears? How are beverages developed? How do brands decide what belongs on a menu? How do you balance creativity with cost and operational reality?

These questions moved me towards product development, NPD and the wider business side of food and beverage.

Learning doesn't have to stay inside one field.

The more I learned, the more I realised that different disciplines connect with each other.

Coffee connects with science. Beverage development connects with operations. Product development connects with consumer behaviour. Business connects all of them.

That is what makes learning interesting to me — one subject keeps opening the door to another.

Keep exploring.

I don't think moving beyond coffee means leaving coffee behind.

It means taking everything coffee has taught me and applying those lessons somewhere new.

For me, the next chapter is about continuing to learn, experiment, build and understand how ideas become real products and experiences.

— GURPREET SINGH
