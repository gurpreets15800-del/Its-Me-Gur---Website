A PROJECT CLOSE TO ME

Sipsters & Co.

Sector 40 • Gurugram

The idea of having a café had been with me for a long time. But before pursuing it, I wanted to understand my industry better — coffee, beverages, operations and the business behind them.

So I kept working, moving through different roles and looking for experiences that would teach me something new. While moving from FirstCoffee to Bistro, I also started discovering a different side of myself — becoming more interested in technology, learning and studying beyond my immediate work.

One evening, I went to meet one of my closest friends for a drink. We started talking about cafés, beverages and the kind of place we would actually want to spend our evenings in. That conversation eventually became the beginning of Sipsters & Co.
